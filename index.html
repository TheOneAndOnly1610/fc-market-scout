How to use it

  1. Open the official EA market in the game or Companion/Web App.
  2. Look for a player listing and enter its name, rating and buy-now price here.
  3. Use the matching list to check your chosen filters.
  4. If you decide to buy, do it yourself in the official EA interface.

This app does not connect to EA, fetch live listings, sign in to your account, or buy cards. It stores entries only in this browser on this device.
